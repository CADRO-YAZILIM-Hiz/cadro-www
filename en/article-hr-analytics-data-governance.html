Data Quality, Ownership, and Governance in HR Analytics

Summary: Even the most advanced HR model cannot produce trustworthy results from incorrect, incomplete, or inconsistently defined data. Governance combines ownership, definitions, access, retention, and decision responsibility.

Dimensions of data quality

  • Accuracy, completeness, freshness, consistency, and traceability.

Ownership matters

The technical system owner is not always the semantic data owner. A data owner manages access and quality; a metric owner manages the formula; a decision owner manages actions based on the output.

The HR data dictionary

Terms such as active employee, turnover, time-to-hire, and absence need a shared definition. Record formula, denominator, period, exceptions, source, owner, and refresh date.

Governance controls

  1. Classification and role-based access.
  2. Quality rules and alerts.
  3. Version and change records.
  4. Retention, deletion, and anonymization schedule.
  5. Human review of reports and model outputs.
  6. Error, challenge, and correction process.

Conclusion

Trust in HR analytics starts with governance before visualization. Data without a clear definition, owner, access policy, and lifecycle can create false certainty instead of strategic insight.
