QR-Based Attendance Tracking in Remote and Hybrid Work

Summary: Hybrid attendance should manage agreed work rhythms, responsibilities, and exceptions—not continuously monitor people. QR can fit limited, clearly defined scenarios.

Attendance is not productivity

A scan may show presence at a place and time; it does not prove contribution or performance. Keeping this distinction prevents the system from becoming punitive surveillance.

Proportionate design

  1. Document purpose and decision use.
  2. Record necessary events instead of continuous location.
  3. Limit monitoring outside work time.
  4. Do not make attendance the sole performance or disciplinary evidence.
  5. Provide explanation, correction, and challenge routes.

Conclusion

Good hybrid time tracking balances the work agreement, operational needs, and employee privacy rather than recording every movement.
