How US Paycheck Taxes Work

Federal Income Tax

7 brackets from 10% to 37%. Standard deduction: $15,000 (single) / $30,000 (married). Progressive rates based on taxable income.

FICA (Social Security + Medicare)

Social Security: 6.2% on first $168,600. Medicare: 1.45% on all wages + 0.9% above $200K. Total FICA is 7.65% up to the wage base.

State & Local Taxes

Vary by state: 0% (TX, FL, WA, NV) to 13.3% (CA). Some cities add local tax (e.g., NYC ~3.9%). This calculator estimates state withholding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 2026 standard deduction?

$15,000 for single filers, $30,000 for married filing jointly, and $22,500 for head of household. This reduces your taxable income before tax is calculated.

How does 401(k) contribution affect my take-home pay?

Traditional 401(k) contributions are pre-tax, reducing your taxable income. For example, 6% contribution on $85,000 = $5,100 tax-deferred, lowering your taxable income to $79,900.

Is Social Security tax capped?

Yes, the 2026 Social Security wage base is $168,600. Wages above this are not subject to the 6.2% Social Security tax, but Medicare tax (1.45%) applies to all wages.
