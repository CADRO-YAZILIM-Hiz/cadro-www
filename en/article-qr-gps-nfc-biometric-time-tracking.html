QR, GPS, NFC, and Biometrics: Comparing Time-Tracking Technologies

Summary: Selecting a time-tracking technology is not simply selecting the strongest verification method. Operations, mobility, security, integration, cost, and privacy risk must be considered together.

Technology choices

  • QR: low hardware cost and quick deployment, but shared-code and proxy-scan risks need expiry, session, location, and anomaly controls.
  • GPS: useful for field operations, but continuous location history creates proportionality, accuracy, battery, and privacy concerns.
  • NFC: fast card or tag verification, with card-sharing, loss, hardware, and shift-density considerations.
  • Biometrics: strong identity binding but higher legal, security, and irreversibility risk; less intrusive alternatives should be assessed first.

Decision criteria

Compare authentication, total cost, indoor and field fit, offline behavior, data type and retention risk, payroll integration, employee experience, and challenge handling.

Conclusion

The best technology is not the one collecting the most data. It reliably serves the purpose, limits unnecessary monitoring, and produces auditable records for payroll.
