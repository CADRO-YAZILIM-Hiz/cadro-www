How It Works

Progressive Tax Bands

20% on £12,571-£50,270, 40% on £50,271-£125,140, 45% above £125,140. First £12,570 is tax-free (Personal Allowance).

National Insurance

Employees pay 8% on earnings between £242-£967/week, and 2% above that. Below £242/week is NI-free.

Pension Auto-Enrolment

UK employers must enroll eligible workers into a pension. Minimum total contribution is 8% (5% employee + 3% employer).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is UK Income Tax calculated?

UK Income Tax uses a progressive system. For 2026/27, the Personal Allowance is £12,570 (tax-free). Basic rate: 20% on £12,571-£50,270. Higher rate: 40% on £50,271-£125,140. Additional rate: 45% above £125,140.

What are the UK National Insurance rates for 2026/27?

Employees pay Class 1 NI: 0% on earnings below £242/week, 8% on £242-£967/week, and 2% above £967/week. Employers pay 13.8% above £175/week.

How does pension contribution affect take-home pay?

Workplace pension contributions are typically deducted before tax (salary sacrifice) or after tax (net pay arrangement). Our calculator assumes standard auto-enrolment at 5% employee contribution.

What is a tax code and how does it affect my pay?

A tax code (like 1257L) tells your employer how much tax-free income you have. The standard code is 1257L (£12,570). Different codes may apply if you have multiple jobs or benefits.
