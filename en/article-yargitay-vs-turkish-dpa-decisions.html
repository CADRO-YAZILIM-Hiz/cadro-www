Yargıtay Decisions vs. Turkish DPA Decisions: What Is the Difference?

Summary: Yargıtay judicial decisions and Turkish DPA decisions are not the same source type. Yargıtay assesses disputes before it; the DPA acts within personal-data protection administration and oversight.

Different questions

  • Yargıtay: proof of working time, overtime, and reliability of records.
  • Turkish DPA: controller, purpose, legal basis, notice, access, retention, security, and data-subject rights.

One event, two assessments

A time record may be evidence in a labor dispute and still require separate privacy analysis. A favorable assessment in one area does not remove obligations in the other.

Publishing checklist

  1. Find the official decision URL.
  2. Verify date, chamber or authority, and decision number.
  3. Separate facts from the holding.
  4. Label broader interpretation as interpretation.
  5. Monitor later legislation and decisions.

Conclusion

Reliable content distinguishes evidence analysis from data-protection compliance and clearly states source type, jurisdiction, and case limits.
