Frequently Asked Questions

Can HR software ROI really be measured?

Yes. The most tangible ROI sources are directly measurable: hours saved from manual processes × HR staff hourly cost = direct labor savings. Additionally, error reduction (incorrect payroll, missed leave tracking), faster hiring, and employee satisfaction are indirect gains.

How does this calculator work?

The tool uses an estimation model based on your entered employee count, HR team size, and manual process hours. Calculations use fixed coefficients derived from SHRM, Deloitte, and CADRO's anonymized customer data. Results are estimates; actual values may vary based on operational differences.

How is CADRO pricing factored into the ROI calculation?

The payback period is calculated using the CADRO Pro plan monthly fixed cost as reference. For current pricing, visit the pricing page. Contact our team for a personalized quote based on your employee count.

Which HR processes deliver the highest ROI?

Time & attendance management (8-12 hours/week savings), leave approval workflows (85% faster), and the ATS module (35% faster position closure) deliver the highest returns. Performance management (OKR) generates indirect ROI over the long term through engagement and retention.
