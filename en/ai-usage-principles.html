RESPONSIBLE AI

Safety and accountability principles for AI use

Use AI with a clear purpose, minimum necessary data, verification, and human responsibility.

Do

  • Define the purpose and expected output before using AI.
  • Keep personal and sensitive data out of unauthorized public tools.
  • Verify outputs before using them in payroll, legal, hiring, or employee decisions.
  • Record human review for high-impact decisions.
  • Report inaccurate, biased, or unsafe outputs.

Do not

  • Turn an AI output into a hiring, promotion, pay, or termination decision without human review.
  • Present unverified output as an official source or research finding.
  • Hide uncertainty, limitations, or model errors.

Quick checklist

  1. Is AI necessary for this task?
  2. Is the data minimized?
  3. Who verifies the output?
  4. How will the process stop if bias or error is found?
  5. Can an affected person appeal?
