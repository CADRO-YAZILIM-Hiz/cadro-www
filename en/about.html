ABOUT CADRO

Modern HR operations, designed for clarity

CADRO is a cloud-based HR software platform for companies that want to move from fragmented spreadsheets and manual follow-up to connected, measurable HR operations.

Our mission

We help HR teams manage employee data, hiring, leave, attendance, payroll, performance and reporting in one practical operating layer.

Our approach

CADRO combines local HR requirements with a multilingual product experience, clear workflows, controlled access and responsible use of data.

What matters to us

  • Data protection, access control and accountable operations.
  • Simple workflows that reduce repetitive manual work.
  • Human review for high-impact decisions and responsible AI use.
  • Continuous improvement based on customer and operational feedback.

Built in Türkiye, ready for growing teams

CADRO was founded in Ankara and serves Turkish, English, German and Arabic audiences. The platform is designed for SMEs and growing organizations that need a reliable HR foundation.
