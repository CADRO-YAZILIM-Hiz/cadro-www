QR Time Tracking, Payroll Integration, and Audit Trails

Summary: Sending time events to payroll is not the same as turning raw scans into pay decisions. Reliable integration combines a data contract, validation, exception handling, authorized approval, and change history.

Three integration layers

  1. Event layer: scan, time, user, shift, and source.
  2. Attendance layer: pairings, breaks, overtime, missing records, and rules.
  3. Payroll layer: approved attendance transferred to pay calculations.

Exception handling

Missing entries, overlapping shifts, suspicious scans, unapproved overtime, and period mismatches need a review queue. Exceptions should not become automatic deductions; an authorized reviewer should record the reason, employee explanation, and result.

Audit trail questions

  • When and from where did the original event arrive?
  • Who changed which value and why?
  • Who approved the change?
  • Which payroll period received it?
  • How was an error or challenge resolved?

Conclusion

QR-to-payroll integration is an evidence chain for wage decisions, not a simple data transfer. Without a data contract, human approval, and history, speed does not mean reliability.
