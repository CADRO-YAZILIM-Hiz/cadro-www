2026 Minimum Wage FAQ

How often is the minimum wage updated?

Previously updated twice yearly before 2022, the minimum wage has recently been updated semi-annually or annually depending on inflation. The exact schedule is determined by the Minimum Wage Determination Commission.

Can employers pay below minimum wage?

No. Employers cannot pay below the minimum wage. Paying below the minimum wage leads to legal sanctions.

What is the difference between minimum wage and base wage?

Base wage refers only to the basic wage amount, excluding meal, transportation, or other social benefits. The minimum wage sets the minimum base wage threshold.
