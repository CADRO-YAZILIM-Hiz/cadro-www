KVKK-Compliant Data Minimization in QR Time Tracking

Summary: QR time tracking should create the attendance event needed for a defined purpose, not accumulate unnecessary location, device, or behavior profiles.

Define purpose and legal context

Document which records are needed for payroll or shift management and which data will not be collected. Future speculation is not a reason to collect unlimited data.

Five design principles

  1. Collect events rather than defaulting to continuous monitoring.
  2. Use role-based, least-privilege access.
  3. Limit retention by purpose and data type.
  4. Operate deletion, destruction, and anonymization schedules.
  5. Provide notice, correction, and application routes.

Anomalies and suppliers

Anomaly records can also be personal data. A “suspicious” label is not automatically a violation or discipline result. Limit review access, record reasons, measure false positives, and assess supplier processors, storage, access logs, deletion, and breach procedures.

Conclusion

KVKK-compliant QR tracking processes the minimum necessary data in a reliable, access-controlled, limited, and auditable way.
