FAQ

Common pricing questions

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades apply at the end of your billing cycle. No downtime occurs during plan changes and your data is preserved.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Choosing the annual billing cycle saves you 20% compared to monthly pricing. The annual plan includes all features with no difference other than the payment period. You can switch to monthly billing at any time.

Can I add more users?

All plans support unlimited users. We use a fixed pricing model that adjusts with user count. As your team grows, you can upgrade your plan to continue accessing all modules.

Is there a free trial?

The Basic plan is completely free with no time limit. For Pro and Enterprise plans, we offer a 14-day free trial. During the trial, you can test all features and decide later.
