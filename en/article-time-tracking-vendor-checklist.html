A Legal and Security Checklist for Choosing a Time-Tracking Vendor

Summary: Vendor selection is not just about scan speed or a mobile interface. Assess processing roles, security architecture, integration integrity, retention, and audit trails together.

Contract and processing

  • Are controller and processor roles defined?
  • Are purpose, categories, retention, subprocessors, and transfer locations documented?
  • What happens to data after termination?

Security and integrity

  • Encryption, access controls, MFA, admin logs.
  • Backups, disaster recovery, incident response.
  • Unique IDs, replay control, error queue, and change history.

Product and operations

Assess offline behavior, shifts, breaks, overtime, employee corrections, role-based reporting, updates, support, and service levels.

Conclusion

A good vendor makes purpose, integrity, access, lifecycle, and contestability manageable—not merely the time event.
